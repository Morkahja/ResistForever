# Resist Forever

Partial-resist chances (10% damage steps) and base spell miss on the character and pet panel resistance tooltips in World of Warcraft: Forever.

Author: BabuniGaming

Hover Fire, Nature, Frost, Shadow, or Arcane resistance on your character or pet. The game's own tooltip stays in place; Resist Forever appends direct-spell partial-resist odds and base spell miss from level difference.

Extract the folder into `Interface\AddOns` and enable **Resist Forever**.
